week 10 field notes
I think it's more fun when you can pull information from other sources into you website. I think sometimes you also get things you don't expect. This week, I wanted to make the experience more fun by incorporating API. 

I used Wikipedia API for info on different birthstone, and people that shares similar birthday with you. I also asked NASA’s Astronomy Picture of the Day. NASA has posted one space photo every day since June 1995. It sends back that day’s photo, title and description. If someone was born before 1995, the page shows the photo from their latest birthday instead.
